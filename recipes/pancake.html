Pancake

Description

This is an easy to make pancake recipe using less ingredient.

The steps provided here are easy to follow, and will make you look like a pro, to create the most delicious pancake ever made.

Ingredients

  • 1-Cup of flour
  • 1/2-Teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1/4-Teaspoon of salt
  • 2-Tablespoon of sugar
  • 2-Ripe bananas
  • 1-Large egg
  • 1-Cup of milk
  • 1-Teaspoon of vanilla
  • 3-tablespoons of butter

Steps

  1. Pour the cup of flour in a bowl, add 1/2 a teaspoon of baking powder, then 1/4 of a teaspoon of salt, and 2-tablespoons of sugar and whisk them all to combine;
  2. Mash 2-ripe bananas in a bowl, add 1-large egg, add a cup of milk, add a teaspoon of vanilla, and then 3-tablespoons of melted butter and mix;
  3. Combine both the dry and wet mixtures together, hit the pan and rub some butter on it. Pour the pancake batter in the pan, and cook each side for 1 min over a low flame.
